# Studies before artistic commitment — 9 September 2026

The user's assessment distinguishes conceptual exploration from discovery through making. Connecting the *Veranda Post* to agency and collective dependence, then proposing a crown, is an interpretive move. It does not establish that the proposal has been tested by making and inspecting alternatives. The supplied burden, construction and collective-structure examples are illustrative possibilities, not prescribed motifs or winning answers.

## New-run behavior

This iteration introduced `independent-research-v14-provisional-studies` and `research-render-v4-study-development`. These version-specific defaults describe the initial bounded iteration; see the concurrent-change verification boundary below. New panel runs enable study development and default to three distinct studies, one image each, with at most one subsequent image. The research `commit` action freezes a **provisional experiment for rendering**; artistic selection occurs later.

1. The exact model-authored `composition` and `render_instruction` are joined by one newline and passed to FLUX. Both components remain inspectable. A shared composer is used for current research admission and new rendering plans, including newly prepared renders from historical research. New follow-up instructions use the same rule. Prompts exceeding the frozen tokenizer allowance are rejected, never truncated. Existing frozen plans are not migrated or retried.
2. Each provisional study requires an `artistic_operation`, a `study_reason` explaining what seeing it might reveal or change, and `distinct_from_previous`. Prior study operations and reasons stay in the research context. Exact duplicate operations, compositions and transformations are rejected. Seed variants do not count as distinct studies. These are operator-authored workflow checks: wording differences do not prove meaningful artistic differences.
3. Every available study image receives a separate frozen-Qwen inspection of its actual saved pixels: visible relationships, unexpected effects, explanation gaps, uncertainty and a possible next question. Empty surprise/gap lists are allowed. The proposal is presented as an intention, never as evidence of what appeared.
4. Selection requires at least three realized studies, all planned initial images, and valid inspections. Qwen sees the actual study images and their observations, cites each image, states its own commitments and what seeing changed, and chooses `continue`, `combine`, `abandon`, `reformulate` or `stop`. Missing or failed work cannot silently become a preference or selection.
5. Continue uses one actual parent. Combine uses the first of two or three selected images as the actual parent and the others as ordered references. Reformulate may use selected images or begin afresh, including abandoning the original symbol. A rendering decision supplies the complete new question, composition and instruction. The one follow-up image is inspected with its selected studies. Abandon and stop make no further image. The bounded sequence ends there; further development remains open.

No crown example or other preferred artistic operation is inserted into the model policy. Source research remains available as evidence; a reformulation does not inherit earlier transferred-property claims as claims about its new proposal.

## Budgets, evidence and interface

For `S` research actions, `D` distinct-study slots and `V` seed variants per study, the new panel ceiling is `S + D*V + 2` Qwen calls and `D*V + 1` FLUX calls. Defaults are 29 Qwen calls (237,568 reserved tokens) and four FLUX images, 33 total calls. The named 512 × 640 study profile and 120-minute window remain explicit. Model choice may stop earlier; the ceiling is not a target.

Separate `study_development: false` API/CLI requests retain rendering diagnostics and the existing optional geometry judge; the panel's full-run form uses study development. The earlier geometry PASS/FAIL report remains a separate diagnostic and is not an artistic selector. The new qualitative observations do not manufacture numeric predicate measurements or satisfy that legacy geometry gate.

All stages share the admitted resource ledger, workload lock, deadline and stop control. Study prompts are counted with the pinned cached Qwen processor before model loading or reservation. No statement, observation, schema repair or renderer retry is synthesized after rejection. Missing charged responses stop for reconciliation. Resume reuses saved responses and images without additional dispatch; panel runs still disallow automatic redispatch.

The render tree contains `study_development/decision-*.json`, `admission-*.json`, `response-*.json`, its authoritative `jobs.sqlite3`, an inspectable projected `report.json`, and optional `followup/` rendering records. The follow-up freezes its selected parent bytes/hash, ordered references, full prompt, model-decision hash and original research-plan identity. Initial images, source evidence, failures and original requests remain intact. The run overview shows study reasons, inspections, the decision and follow-up inspection. The image gallery shows the actual new outputs without counting the copied parent again. Cost scopes include the study calls and follow-up render.

All interpretations and selections are attributed to frozen Qwen (or explicit test actors). No human votes, advancement, training or learned artistic advantage is created. Local evidence remains outside Git and requires its preserved artifact root in another checkout.
